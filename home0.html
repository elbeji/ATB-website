Our Partners

We collaborate with industry leaders to deliver exceptional solutions to our clients.

TrustNoted

TrustNoteD is a Switzerland-based global solutions provider specializing in digital innovations for Central Bank Digital Currency (CBDC), tokenization, and Distributed Ledger Technology (DLT)-based identity solutions. They offers services, including consulting, implementation, and operation, tailored for governments, financial institutions, and organizations

Orell Fussli

Orell Füssli specializes in high-security printing and is a leading provider of secure banknotes and identification documents for central banks and issuing authorities worldwide. Their expertise includes designing and producing passports, visas, ID cards, and driving licenses, incorporating advanced security features. They also focus on innovative identity management solutions and digital transformation efforts, such as mobile driving licenses. The company combines centuries of printing tradition with cutting-edge technology to deliver exceptional security and quality

Navayo

Navayo Group is a cutting-edge IT research and development company specializing in hardware-based encryption solutions for secure communications.They offers a comprehensive ecosystem of security and privacy products designed for governmental and military clients across four continents

Fraunhofer Institute

The Fraunhofer-Gesellschaft, headquartered in Germany, is one of the world's leading organizations for applied research. Established in 1949, it focuses on conducting cutting-edge research and transferring results to industry to strengthen Germany's industrial base and benefit society as a whole.

ChebCheb Group

CHEBCHEB Group GmbH is an Austrian-based company specializing in the design, production, and distribution of specialized vehicles and comprehensive project management services. With a strong presence in both Europe and North Africa, the company bridges Austrian engineering excellence with deep regional expertise.

MDS16

​MDS 16 is a Turkish defense and aerospace company headquartered in Istanbul, specializing in advanced military systems, space technologies, and cybersecurity solutions. The company offers integrated, technology-driven services tailored to the needs of land, naval, air, and space defense operations.​ .

Baus-AT

​B.A.U.S. AT. is a leading manufacturer of ambulances and specialized emergency vehicles,the company combines German management expertise with Polish craftsmanship to deliver high-quality, reliable vehicles. Through its commitment to quality and customer satisfaction, B.A.U.S. AT has established itself as a trusted partner in the emergency vehicle manufacturing industry

Hartmann

​Hartmann Spezialkarosserien GmbH, based in Alsfeld, Germany, specializes in designing and manufacturing custom vehicles tailored to diverse sectors. With a legacy dating back to 1909, the company combines traditional craftsmanship with modern technology to deliver high-quality, individualized solutions.​ .

Blectec

BlecTec GmbH, headquartered in Jessen (Elster), Saxony-Anhalt, Germany, is a privately held company specializing in metal processing, special machine construction, and custom vehicle manufacturing. With a history dating back to 1941, BlecTec has established itself as a versatile provider of comprehensive solutions in the metalworking industry.
