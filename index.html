Our Partners

We collaborate with industry leaders to deliver exceptional solutions to our clients.

TrustNoted

TrustNoteD is a Switzerland-based global solutions provider specializing in digital innovations for Central Bank Digital Currency (CBDC), tokenization, and Distributed Ledger Technology (DLT)-based identity solutions. They offers services, including consulting, implementation, and operation, tailored for governments, financial institutions, and organizations

Orell Fussli

Orell Füssli specializes in high-security printing and is a leading provider of secure banknotes and identification documents for central banks and issuing authorities worldwide. Their expertise includes designing and producing passports, visas, ID cards, and driving licenses, incorporating advanced security features.

Navayo

Navayo Group is a cutting-edge IT research and development company specializing in hardware-based encryption solutions for secure communications. They offers a comprehensive ecosystem of security and privacy products designed for governmental and military clients across four continents

Fraunhofer Institute

The Fraunhofer-Gesellschaft, headquartered in Germany, is one of the world's leading organizations for applied research. Established in 1949, it focuses on conducting cutting-edge research and transferring results to industry to strengthen Germany's industrial base and benefit society as a whole.

ChebCheb Group

CHEBCHEB Group GmbH is an Austrian-based company specializing in the design, production, and distribution of specialized vehicles and comprehensive project management services. With a strong presence in both Europe and North Africa, the company bridges Austrian engineering excellence with deep regional expertise.

MDS16

MDS 16 is a Turkish defense and aerospace company headquartered in Istanbul, specializing in advanced military systems, space technologies, and cybersecurity solutions. The company offers integrated, technology-driven services tailored to the needs of land, naval, air, and space defense operations.
